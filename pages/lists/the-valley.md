The game is set in one continuous valley: a village on the west bank of a river, roads and lanes, a stone bridge and a ford, and forest all round. Beyond the valley's edge the wild forest goes on for ever, grown as you walk, with a few of the dead in it.

{{valley-map}}

Map north is up and to the right, as on screen: "map north" is the same direction on every player's screen.
