A **wheel of cheese** is [[Food]]. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.cheese.hunger}} and heals {{n items.cheese.heal}}. It turns up in chests, on shelves and in cupboards, and on the inn's counter (each table's odds are on [[Drop tables]]), and it is made at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) from jugs of milk, which a fed cow gives (see [[Jug of milk]] and [[Keeping]]). It takes a cell of its own and does not stack.

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
