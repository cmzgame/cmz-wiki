The **leather belt** (the good belt until the names were made specific) is equipped at the waist and gives {{n items.good_belt.beltSlots}} belt slots, pressed with the number keys (see [[Belt]]), against the [[Rope belt]]'s {{n items.belt.beltSlots}}. The valley's one leather belt hangs on the tool rack in [[Hollow Farm]]'s great barn, with a pair of [[Work gloves]].

Equipping it swaps the old belt into your bag. Empty, it can be carried in a bag; with anything on it, it cannot.
