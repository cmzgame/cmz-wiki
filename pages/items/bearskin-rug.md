The **bearskin rug** is a [[Rugs|rug]]: a bear's hide spread flat, carried rolled up in your bag and laid on the floor with **Place** on its menu. Laid, it lies under everything, you walk over it, and the furniture you can carry may stand on it; **Pick up** rolls it back into the bag. Nothing makes one yet and nothing in the valley holds one: it is in the [[Developer chest]], the one rug left there, and can be added to a new world's Extra starting items. How to lay it, turn it, and pick it up again is on [[Rugs]].
