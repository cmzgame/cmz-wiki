The **pickaxe** is a tool made at the [[Anvil]] from [[Iron bar|iron bars]] and a [[Stick]]. Nothing in the valley gives iron bars yet, so it cannot be made in play, and it is not found anywhere. It can be held in either hand and swung as a weak weapon that wears out in {{n items.pickaxe.weapon.kills}} kills.
