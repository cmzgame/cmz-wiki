The **millstones** stand in [[The mill]]. They grind [[Grain]] into [[Flour]] for the [[Oven]]'s bread and pie. They need no fire and no tool. Grain comes from the rye of [[Long Acre]], cut by hand or with a [[Scythe]], and rye seed is in the granaries of [[Hollow Farm]] and the mill.

## Using it

Right-click them and choose their entry, or press `E` beside them, and pick the recipe (see [[Crafting]]). The flour goes into their output box.
