The **millstones** stand in [[The mill]]. They grind [[Grain]] into [[Flour]] for the [[Oven]]'s bread and pie. They need no fire and no tool. Grain comes from the rye of [[Long Acre]], harvested with a [[Scythe]] on you, or a row at a swing with one in hand, and rye seed is in the granaries of [[Hollow Farm]] and the mill.

## Using it

Right-click them and choose their entry, or press `E` beside them, and pick the recipe (see [[Crafting]]). The flour goes into their output box.

## Village stations

The village's millstones stays where it stands, and none is made, unless the world was made with the rule **Village stations** set to Movable and craftable (see [[World rules]]). Then it can be picked up and carried like furniture, and another millstones at the [[Workbench]]; one a player made works as the village's does.

## With friends

A guest grinds through the host like any station.
