{{infobox-item belt}}

The **rope belt** is worn at the waist and gives {{n items.belt.beltSlots}} belt slots, pressed with the number keys (see [[Belt]]). Every new character wears one. The [[Good belt]], from chests, has more slots.

Empty, it can be carried in a bag; with anything on it, it cannot.
