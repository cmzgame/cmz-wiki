**Woollen yarn** is spun from [[Wool]] at the [[Spinning wheel]] in [[The weaver]]'s workroom. Its one use is the [[Loom]], which weaves it into [[Woollen cloth]]. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art.

The game's design names Crafting for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
