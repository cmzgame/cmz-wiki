**Plain pottage** is the meatless pottage, made at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) from [[Potato|potatoes]], a [[Cabbage]], and [[Garlic]], all of which can be grown from the first day (see [[Farming]]). Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.stew.hunger}} and thirst by {{n items.stew.thirst}} and heals {{n items.stew.heal}}, as much as any food heals. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack. In the bag its short label is "Pottage".

It was named **Stew** until the animals' recipes came, when the [[Meat pottage]] and the [[Hearty stew]] were made beside it; a world saved before keeps its pottage under the new name.
