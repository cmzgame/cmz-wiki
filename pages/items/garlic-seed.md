A **garlic seed** is planted in tilled soil to grow garlic (see [[Farming]]). Its harvest gives [[Garlic]] and {{n crops.crops.garlic.seeds}} garlic seeds back, harvested with a [[Shovel]] on you. It stacks to {{n items.garlic_seed.stack}}. It can be had in play: the granaries of [[Hollow Farm]] and [[The mill]] hold the first, and a harvest gives seeds back.
