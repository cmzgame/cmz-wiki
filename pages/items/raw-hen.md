**Raw hen** is a hen's [[Meat|meat]]. Butchering a hen's carcass with a [[Dirk]] carried gives {{n animals.species.hen.yields.butcher.0.count.0}} as a whole yield, at the share the kill left (see [[Hunting]]). Its carcass gives this and its [[Feather|feathers]]; a hen has no hide. It is food, eaten as it is: it fills hunger by {{n items.hen_raw.hunger}} and heals {{n items.hen_raw.heal}}.

Cooked alone at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) it becomes a [[Roast hen]], which fills hunger by {{n items.hen_cooked.hunger}} and heals {{n items.hen_cooked.heal}}. As any meat it goes into the [[Meat pottage]], the [[Hearty stew]], a [[Meat pie]], [[Smoked meat]], and [[Pemmican]] (see [[Meat#Cooking it|Meat]]).
