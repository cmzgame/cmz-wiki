**Raw hen** is a hen's [[Meat|meat]]. Butchering a hen's carcass with a [[Dirk]] carried gives {{n animals.species.hen.yields.butcher.0.count.0}} as a whole yield, at the share the kill left (see [[Hunting]]). Its carcass gives this and its [[Feather|feathers]]; a hen has no hide. It is food, eaten as it is: it fills hunger by {{n items.hen_raw.hunger}} and heals {{n items.hen_raw.heal}}. Nothing cooks it yet.
