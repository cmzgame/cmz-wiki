An **unfired jar** is clay shaped into a jar but not yet fired. The [[Workbench]] shapes one from one [[Clay]]: a jar is bigger than a vial, so one clay makes one jar where it makes two [[Unfired vial|unfired vials]]. It has one use: fired in an [[Oven]] over its lit fire, it becomes a [[Clay jar]], the empty jar a [[Beehive|hive]]'s honey is taken into. It is its own item so that firing it in the oven is a step of its own. It stacks to {{n items.unfired_jar.stack}} in one cell.

Its card reads "Unfired jar", its kind "Material", that it is made at a workbench, and that it goes into the clay jar. It is on placeholder art, the unfired vial's own design in its own colour, until its own art comes.

{{item-sections unfired_jar}}

## With friends

A guest shapes and fires jars at the workbench and the oven through the station panel's own commands, like any recipe; the host takes the clay or the jar from that guest.
