The **smith's hammer** (the hammer until the names were made specific) is made at the [[Anvil]] from an [[Iron bar]] and a [[Stick]], and is the one anvil recipe that needs no hammer in hand. Every other anvil recipe needs it held. Nothing in the valley gives iron bars yet, so it cannot be made in play, and it is not found anywhere. It can be held in either hand and swung as a weak weapon that breaks after about {{n items.hammer.weapon.kills}} kills.
