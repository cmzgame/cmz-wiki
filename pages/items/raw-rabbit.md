**Raw rabbit** is a rabbit's [[Meat|meat]]. Butchering a rabbit's carcass with a [[Dirk]] carried gives {{n animals.species.rabbit.yields.butcher.0.count.0}} as a whole yield, at the share the kill left (see [[Hunting]]). A [[Snare]]'s catch is a rabbit too, at its whole yield. It is food, eaten as it is: it fills hunger by {{n items.rabbit_raw.hunger}} and heals {{n items.rabbit_raw.heal}}. Nothing cooks it yet.
