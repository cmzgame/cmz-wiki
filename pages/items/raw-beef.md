**Raw beef** is a cow's [[Meat|meat]]. Butchering a cow's carcass with a [[Dirk]] carried gives {{n animals.species.cow.yields.butcher.0.count.0}}–{{n animals.species.cow.yields.butcher.0.count.1}} as a whole yield, at the share the kill left (see [[Hunting]]). A cow is tame stock: a blow strikes one only when nothing else is in reach (see [[Hunting]]). It is food, eaten as it is: it fills hunger by {{n items.beef_raw.hunger}} and heals {{n items.beef_raw.heal}}. Nothing cooks it yet.
