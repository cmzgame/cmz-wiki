**Butter** is churned from a [[Jug of milk]] at the [[Workbench]], with no tool and no fire. It is [[Food]], if a small one: eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.butter.hunger}} and heals {{n items.butter.heal}}. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art. No recipe takes it yet; [[Pemmican]] is bound with [[Lard]] or honey, not butter.

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
