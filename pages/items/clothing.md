**Clothing** is anything worn. The character screen (`C`) has seven worn slots: **head**, **chest**, **hands** (gloves), **legs**, **feet**, **waist** (the [[Belt]]), and **back** (the bag; see [[Inventory]]). Every garment names its one slot.

## How it works

### Wearing

**Wear** is on a garment's menu in your bag or an open container, and on a garment lying on the floor. Dragging a garment onto the wrong slot is refused. Wearing over a filled slot swaps the old garment into your bag; with no room the game says "No room to take the old one off." A new bag on the back drops the old bag at your feet with everything in it.

**Take off** on a worn slot's menu puts the garment in your bag, or on the floor if the bag is full ("No room in your bag; it is on the floor."). Taking off the bag sets it down.

### What clothes do

Clothes do nothing beyond being worn and drawn on your figure. A belt gives belt slots and a bag gives a grid; the rest are for looks. Every garment except the belts and bags is cloth, which some recipes take (see [[Crafting]]).

A new character wears a [[Tunic]], [[Breeches]], [[Boots]], a [[Rope belt]], and a [[Burlap sack]], with the head bare. [[Gloves]] and a [[Hood]] turn up in chests. The dead wear clothes too, and searching a body lays them out to be taken.

## With friends

Wearing and taking off are sent to the host and carried out for that player, and what each player wears is drawn for everyone.
