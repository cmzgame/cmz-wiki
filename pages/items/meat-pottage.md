**Meat pottage** is the pottage with meat, made beside the meatless [[Plain pottage]] at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]). It takes any raw [[Meat|meat]] (a raw hen, rabbit, venison, beef, mutton, or pork, not [[Bacon]]), [[Potato|potatoes]], and a [[Cabbage]]; the table below has the counts. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.meat_pottage.hunger}} and thirst by {{n items.meat_pottage.thirst}} and heals {{n items.meat_pottage.heal}}, as much as any food heals. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art.

The meat may come from your bag, your [[Belt]], a hand, or a searched container within reach, and it is spent wherever it was taken from (see [[Crafting#Ingredients|Crafting]]).
