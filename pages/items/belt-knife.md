The **belt knife** (the knife until the names were made specific) is a small tool made at the [[Anvil]] from an [[Iron bar]] and a [[Stick]], with a [[Smith's hammer]] in hand. The chest in [[The weaver]] holds one. Carried anywhere, in a hand, on the belt, or in the bag, it lets you tear a garment into [[Rag|rags]] at the [[Workbench]]. It can be held in either hand and swung as a weak weapon that breaks after about {{n items.knife.weapon.kills}} kills.
