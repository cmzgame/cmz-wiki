**Hearty stew** is made at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) from raw [[Meat|meat]] of any kind, two kinds mixed if you like (beef and pork make one as well as beef alone), with a [[Potato]], a [[Cabbage]], and [[Garlic]]. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.hearty_stew.hunger}}, more than any other food, fills thirst by {{n items.hearty_stew.thirst}}, and heals {{n items.hearty_stew.heal}}. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art. In the bag its short label is "Stew".

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
