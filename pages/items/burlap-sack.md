The **burlap sack** is the bag every new character wears on their back. It gives a grid of {{n items.small_sack.bag.0}} × {{n items.small_sack.bag.1}} cells, the smallest of the bags (see [[Inventory]]). The dead carry one too, and searching a body lays out its sack with what is in it. A [[Rucksack]] from a chest holds more.

Wearing another bag drops the sack at your feet with everything in it. Empty, it can be carried inside another bag; full, it cannot.
