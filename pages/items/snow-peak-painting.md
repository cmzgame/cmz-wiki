The **snow peak painting** is a small painting, taking one stretch of wall: a snowy mountain peak, carried in your bag and hung on the face of a plain wall with **Place** on its menu. **Pick up** takes it down into the bag again, and it falls to the floor, whole, if its wall is removed or built over. Nothing makes one yet, and neither the valley nor the [[Developer chest]] holds one: it can be added to a new world's Extra starting items. Where it can hang, and how, is on [[Paintings]].
