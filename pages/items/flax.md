**Flax** is the sixth crop and its harvest. It is grown like the others (see [[Farming]]): plant a [[Flax seed]] in tilled soil, keep it watered, and harvest it when it is ready, for {{n crops.crops.flax.harvest.count.0}} or {{n crops.crops.flax.harvest.count.1}} flax and {{n crops.crops.flax.seeds}} seeds back. The [[Loom]] weaves flax into [[Linen cloth]]. The first flax seed is in the chest of [[The weaver]]. It stacks to {{n items.flax.stack}}.

Flax is not grain: the [[Scythe]] does not cut it.
