A **cabbage seed** is planted in tilled soil to grow cabbage (see [[Farming]]). Its harvest gives [[Cabbage]] and two cabbage seeds back. It stacks to {{n items.cabbage_seed.stack}}. It can be had in play: harvesting a cabbage gives two back, and the plot by [[Your cottage]] has cabbages from the start.
