A **rye seed** is planted in tilled soil to grow rye (see [[Farming]]). Its harvest gives [[Grain]] and {{n crops.crops.rye.seeds}} rye seeds back, harvested with a [[Scythe]] on you. It stacks to {{n items.rye_seed.stack}}. It can be had in play: the granaries of [[Hollow Farm]] and [[The mill]] hold the first, and a harvest gives seeds back.
