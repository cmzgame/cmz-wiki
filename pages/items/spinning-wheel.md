The **spinning wheel** in [[The weaver]]'s workroom spins [[Wool]] into [[Woollen yarn]], which the [[Loom]] beside it weaves into [[Woollen cloth]]. It needs no fire and no tool. It is the only one: no recipe makes a spinning wheel, and it is fixed where it stands, so it cannot be picked up and carried (it could be, before it became a station). See [[Dead ends]].

## Using it

Right-click it and choose its entry, or press `E` beside it, and pick the recipe in the panel (see [[Crafting]]). Wool is shorn from a fed sheep with [[Shears]] (see [[Keeping]]). What it makes goes into the wheel's output box.
