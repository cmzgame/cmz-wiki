**Woollen cloth** is woven from [[Woollen yarn]] at the [[Loom]]. It counts as cloth: every recipe that asks for "any cloth" takes it, the [[Torch]], the [[Fishing rod]], the [[Hunting bow]], the [[Snare]], the [[Lead]], the bed, and the stall among them. The recipes that name [[Linen cloth]] itself do not take it: the [[Bandage]], the linen garments, and the [[Linen satchel]] still want linen. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art.

The game's design names Crafting for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
