**Eggs and bacon** is fried at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) from [[Egg|eggs]], laid by the hens of [[Hollow Farm]], and [[Bacon]], butchered from a pig. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.eggs_and_bacon.hunger}} and heals {{n items.eggs_and_bacon.heal}}. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art. In the bag its short label is "Breakfast"; a line counts it as one thing ("some eggs and bacon").

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
