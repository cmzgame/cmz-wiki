A **flax seed** is planted in tilled soil to grow [[Flax]] (see [[Farming]]). Its harvest gives flax and {{n crops.crops.flax.seeds}} flax seeds back. It stacks to {{n items.flax_seed.stack}}. It can be had in play: the chest in [[The weaver]] and the granaries of [[Hollow Farm]] and [[The mill]] hold the first, and [[Long Acre]]'s flax strip gives seeds back when harvested.
