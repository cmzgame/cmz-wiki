The **scythe** is a tool made at the [[Anvil]] from an [[Iron bar]] and [[Stick|sticks]]. Nothing in the valley gives iron bars yet, so it cannot be made in play, and it is not found anywhere. It can be held in either hand and swung as a weak weapon that breaks after about {{n items.scythe.weapon.kills}} kills.

Crops are harvested by hand today (see [[Farming]]).
