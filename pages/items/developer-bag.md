{{devonly note="The Developer Bag is for testing: it comes from the dev chest and the console."}}

The **Developer Bag** is a bag worn on the back with a grid of {{n items.dev_bag.bag.0}} × {{n items.dev_bag.bag.1}} cells, far bigger than any other (see [[Inventory]]). The [[Developer chest]] holds two, and a wheelbarrow's first search gives two as well. On the console, `__game.giveKit()` puts one on your back with what you carried moved into it, and `__game.giveDevBag()` gives one.
