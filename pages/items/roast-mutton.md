**Roast mutton** is [[Raw mutton|raw mutton]], the meat of a sheep, cooked alone at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]): pick it in a campfire's, a [[Fireplace|fireplace]]'s, a [[Fire pit|fire pit]]'s, or the [[Oven]]'s panel with the raw meat on you or in a searched container within reach. It is [[Food]]: eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.mutton_cooked.hunger}} and heals {{n items.mutton_cooked.heal}}, where the raw meat fills {{n items.mutton_raw.hunger}} and heals {{n items.mutton_raw.heal}}. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art.

Cooking a meat alone is the quick way; the same raw meat goes further in a dish (the [[Meat pottage]], the [[Hearty stew]], a [[Meat pie]], [[Smoked meat]], or [[Pemmican]]). Every meat is on [[Meat]].
