The **castle painting** is a wide painting, spanning two stretches of the same wall side by side: a castle, carried in your bag and hung on the face of a plain wall with **Place** on its menu. **Pick up** takes it down into the bag again, and it falls to the floor, whole, if its wall is removed or built over. Nothing makes one yet and nothing in the valley holds one: it is in the [[Developer chest]], the one painting left there, and can be added to a new world's Extra starting items. Where it can hang, and how, is on [[Paintings]].
