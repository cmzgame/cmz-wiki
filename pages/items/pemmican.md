**Pemmican** is made at a lit fire (see [[Cooking]]) from any raw [[Meat|meat]] (not [[Bacon]]), a plant ([[Garlic]] or a [[Cabbage]]), and a binder ([[Lard]] or a [[Pot of honey]]); one make gives more than one (the table below has the counts). The plant is only those two, so none of the poisonous [[Herbs]] ever goes in. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.pemmican.hunger}} and heals only {{n items.pemmican.heal}}: a stack fills you on the road, but does not heal you through a fight.

It is the one cooked food that stacks, up to {{n items.pemmican.stack}} in a cell. It is on placeholder art.

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
