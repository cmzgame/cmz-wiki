**Smoked meat** is made at a [[Smoking rack]] from any raw [[Meat|meat]] (not [[Bacon]]) and a [[Log]], which the recipe spends to smoke it; the rack needs no lighting. Eaten, it fills hunger by {{n items.smoked_meat.hunger}} and heals {{n items.smoked_meat.heal}}. It takes a cell of its own and does not stack, and it is on placeholder art.

The game's design names Cooking for it; like every skill gate, it is open to all until the skills are built.
