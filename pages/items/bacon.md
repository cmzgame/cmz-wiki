**Bacon** comes only from a pig. Butchering a pig's carcass with a [[Dirk]] carried gives {{n animals.species.pig.yields.butcher.1.count.0}}–{{n animals.species.pig.yields.butcher.1.count.1}} as a whole yield, beside its [[Raw pork|pork]] and [[Lard]], at the share the kill left (see [[Hunting]]). A pig is tame stock, struck only when nothing else is in reach. It is food, eaten as it is: it fills hunger by {{n items.bacon.hunger}} and heals {{n items.bacon.heal}}. Nothing cooks it yet. See [[Meat]] for every meat.
