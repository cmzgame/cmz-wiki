{{infobox-item good_belt}}

The **good belt** is equipped at the waist and gives {{n items.good_belt.beltSlots}} belt slots, pressed with the number keys (see [[Belt]]), against the [[Rope belt]]'s {{n items.belt.beltSlots}}. It is found in chests.

Equipping it swaps the old belt into your bag. Empty, it can be carried in a bag; with anything on it, it cannot.
