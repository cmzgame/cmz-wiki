**Fists** are what the [[Player]] fights with when nothing usable is in the main hand. A punch is weak and short, never floors one of [[The dead]], and never wears out. An item with no weapon block held in the main hand (food, the [[Book of Fire]], the [[Staff of Fire]], the [[Throwing knife]], the [[Throwing axe]], and so on) also swings as fists.

## Combat

Left punches. Fists have no other mode.

The Swing targets setting works with fists too. Fists do not wear (see [[Durability]]). The [[Shove]] works with empty hands as well.
