This guide walks through a first day in [[The valley]], in the order you will meet things. Every system it mentions has its own page.

## Making a world

From the title, open [[Worlds]] and make a new world. Each world asks you to make a [[Characters|character]] for it from an [[Identities|identity]]: a name and a look kept in your browser. Your character belongs to that world alone. A friend can join you later; see [[Multiplayer]].

## Where you start

You wake at hour {{n census.constants.clock.START_HOUR}} of day one (in the morning) in [[Your cottage]], at the southern end of the village: two rooms, a hearth, a bed, chests, a well, and a fenced plot of crops. A full day lasts {{n census.constants.clock.DAY_LENGTH}} seconds of real time (see [[The day]]).

You start with plain clothes, a [[Rope belt]], a [[Burlap sack]] on your back holding a [[Waterskin]], a [[Loaf of bread]], [[Apple|apples]] and a [[Plank]], and a [[Torch]] in your hand. Press **Tab** for your bag and **C** for the character screen.

## The first things to do

1. **Look round the cottage.** Right-click anything for what you can do with it; **E** does the nearest simple thing. Search the [[Containers|chests]].
2. **Learn the doors and shutters.** Tap **E** at a door or a window to open or shut it; hold **E** at an open window to climb through. Shut shutters block sight both ways. See [[Openings]].
3. **Keep fed and watered.** [[Hunger]] and [[Thirst]] fall over the day and show as [[Moodles]] down the right of the screen. Drink at the well or any water, and fill your waterskin there.
4. **Mind your fires.** A fire burns its fuel down over game hours and goes out. Feed it [[Log|logs]] from its menu. See [[Fire]].
5. **Sleep in a bed, indoors,** when you are tired. The night passes quickly while you sleep, and nothing wakes you, so shut the door first. See [[Sleep]].

## The dead

The dead walk slowly, see you by the light you stand in, and remember only where they last saw you. At night they notice a flame from far away, so a torch in your hand is a lantern for them too. Left-click swings what is in your main hand; see [[Combat]] and [[The dead]].
