The game is in development. This page lists what is planned but not built, so the rest of the wiki can describe the game as it is. Each entry names the design document in the game's repository that plans it. When a thing is built, it leaves this page and gets real sections where it belongs.

Every small "Planned" note across the wiki is listed on [[Special:Planned notes|the planned notes page]].

## The big pass (the game's `docs/30`)

The valley's next work was filed as the game's issues #194 to #204; what is left of the big pass:

- **The castle** across the river, then the rest of **the forest places** (the woodcutters, the witch, the hunter, the bandits).
- **Tools' verbs:** the shovel's own digging (a grave, the buried chest, a plot dug up), and the knife's butchering and skinning.
- **Animals:** chickens and deer, leather, the tannery.

Fishing, apple trees, farming, the farms and the mill, the bucket and the scythe, the hatchet's felling, mining, charcoal, cloth, and the tools an action asks for, once planned here, are built: see [[Fishing]], [[Trees]], [[Farming]], [[Hollow Farm]], [[Long Acre]], [[The mill]], [[Bucket]], [[Scythe]], [[Stump]], [[Mining]], [[Charcoal clamp]], [[Linen cloth]], and [[Smith's hammer]].

## Other plans

- **The map on M** (`docs/33`, #203, #204): what anyone in your world has seen, every player's position live.
- **Conversations** (`docs/34`, #202): a panel of a villager's lines and your choices between them. Their barks, and the guide by your cottage, are built: see [[Talk]] and [[Wyn]].
- **Villagers who flee, follow, and stay** (`docs/04`).
- **Infection's effects and cures** (a marker today).
- **Better animations** (`docs/32`): sleeping in a bed, sitting, deaths.
- **Throwing knives and axes, and more magic** (`docs/24`): the [[Fireball]] is built; a cost for casting and other spells are not.
- **More of burning** (`docs/05`): [[Burning]] is built for the dead; the dead noticing it, a way to put a fire out, a player burning, and fire spreading are not.
- **A demo** (`docs/36`).
