The game is in development. This page lists what is planned but not built, so the rest of the wiki can describe the game as it is. Each entry names the design document in the game's repository that plans it. When a thing is built, it leaves this page and gets real sections where it belongs.

Every small "Planned" note across the wiki is listed on [[Special:Planned notes|the planned notes page]].

## The big pass (the game's `docs/30`)

- **Farming:** tilling soil with a hoe, planting seeds, watering, growth over days, harvesting; the bucket; the scythe; grain to flour to bread; the farms and the mill.
- **The castle** across the river, then **the forest places** (the woodcutters, the witch, the quarry, the charcoal burners, the iron workings, the hunter, the bandits).
- **Tools' verbs:** mining with the pickaxe, digging with the shovel, harvesting with the scythe.
- **Animals:** chickens and deer, leather, the tannery.
- **Fishing.**

## Other plans

- **The map on M** (`docs/33`): what anyone in your world has seen, every player's position live.
- **Talk** (`docs/34`): conversations with villagers, and a guide who explains the rules.
- **Villagers who flee, follow, and stay** (`docs/04`).
- **Infection's effects and cures** (a marker today).
- **Better animations** (`docs/32`): sleeping in a bed, sitting, deaths.
- **Throwing knives and axes, and more magic** (`docs/24`): the [[Fireball]] is built; costs, bursts, and other spells are not.
- **A demo** (`docs/36`).
