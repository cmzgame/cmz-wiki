On 2026-10-17 [[The valley]] became the game's focus. This page is everything a player can try that has changed since then, one entry per change, with how to try it and the pages to read. It replaces the page called Valley day. Michael ticks the entries and files bug reports on his own copy of this list, [his playtest checklist](https://claude.ai/artifact/61HoYfV2iwDFboMeAikYws) (private to him); this page is the readable list, kept current with each merge. [[Updates]] has the same changes sync by sync.

**Worth knowing before you play:** with the [[Survival]] setting off, your body counts as always well fed, so health comes back at the well-fed rate, and food still heals (see [[Food]]).

Each area lists its newest changes first. The box at the front of each entry is for ticking on a printout; the page itself keeps no ticks.

## Combat

- ☐ **Throwing knives and axes** (game #494, merged 2026-10-26): Make ten throwing knives (one iron bar) or two axes (a bar and a plank) at the anvil, hold the stack in the main hand, and throw with the left button. They spin in flight; the knife staggers sometimes, the axe always and can floor. Misses lie on the ground; E picks up the whole pile from that tile and its neighbours. Read: [[Throwing knife]], [[Throwing axe]], [[Anvil]].
- ☐ **Dual wielding swings the off arm** (game #450, merged 2026-10-25): Hold a weapon in each hand and alternate swings: the off-hand swing now moves the off arm. Read: [[Dual wielding]].
- ☐ **The shove as the crowd tool** (game #371, #389, #396, merged 2026-10-24): Press Space facing a few of the dead: it pushes up to three back about 0.65 tiles and staggers them, and they reel. It costs 15 stamina and you cannot swing for half a second after. With an empty bar the shove still pushes, about 0.45 tiles. Read: [[Shove]], [[Stamina]], [[Controls]], [[Levers]].
- ☐ **A downed zombie's bar and outline** (game #390, merged 2026-10-24): Knock one of the dead down (mace or shove): its health bar sits low over its body, and pointing at it outlines it as the moment for a finisher. Read: [[The dead]], [[Stagger and knockdown]].
- ☐ **Aim guide on by default** (game #141, #390, merged 2026-10-24): On a fresh browser, draw the bow, a throw, or a cast: the aim guide shows. Settings > Aim guide turns it off for the challenge. #141 turned it off by default; #390 turned it back on. Only the current state is listed. Read: [[Settings]], [[Archery]].
- ☐ **The spear breaks one in four** (game #383, merged 2026-10-24): Throw the spear a few times, hits and misses: about one throw in four breaks it, and you read "The throwing spear breaks." A spear left lodged in a body keeps its wear when you loot it. Read: [[Throwing spear]].
- ☐ **The dead are tougher (150)** (game #376, merged 2026-10-24): Count hits to kill: about 5 with the sword, 4 with the mace, 4 arrows with the bow. A full-draw spear throw still kills. Read: [[The dead]], [[Weapons]].
- ☐ **Stamina paces melee** (game #373, merged 2026-10-24): Swing a sword over and over: each swing costs more than before (sword 10), and the bar refills at 10 a second. Run it dry and the next swing is a tired blow at half damage. A weapon's hover card shows its stamina cost. Read: [[Stamina]], [[Weapons]].
- ☐ **Only some weapons knock down** (game #372, merged 2026-10-24): Fight with the sword: it never floors the dead. The mace (35%), the halberd's thrust (50%), and the shove can. A downed one lies 0.8 s; a hit while it gets up does plain damage, no finisher. Read: [[Stagger and knockdown]], [[Weapons]], [[The dead]].
- ☐ **The bite's wind-up and hit feel** (game #370, merged 2026-10-24): Watch one of the dead attack: it draws back for a beat (about 0.4 s) before the bite lands. Hit it during the wind-up and the bite is cancelled. Every hit makes it flinch with a short hit stop, and the dead in your swing's reach are outlined. Read: [[The dead]], [[Combat]], [[Settings]].

## Magic & potions

- ☐ **Mana twice as fast, its bar under health** (game #514, merged 2026-10-26): Cast a Fireball or two from the Book of Fire with the Staff of Fire and watch the blue bar come back: two points every three seconds (0.667 a second), full from empty in two and a half minutes, where it took five. The bars at the top left now read Health, Mana, Stamina, top to bottom. A guest's bar rises the same. Read: [[Mana]], [[Interface]], [[Levers]].
- ☐ **Poisons on blades and arrows** (game #496, merged 2026-10-26): Brew a poison at a cauldron (2 nightshade, 1 grave moss), then Use it on a blade (sword, dirk, hatchet, scythe, spear) to coat it for ten landed hits, or on plain arrows to make five poisoned arrows. A poisoned zombie or wild animal tints green, pulsing deeper with its stacks (up to three), and ticks damage as floating numbers. Strong poison (wolfsbane) hits harder. Read: [[Poison]], [[Strong poison]], [[Poisoned arrow]], [[Strongly poisoned arrow]].
- ☐ **Vials, the cauldron, drinking potions** (game #491, merged 2026-10-26): Shape clay into an unfired vial at the workbench and fire it in an oven. At the witch's cauldron (north end of the lane to the mere) or your own from the anvil, lit with logs, brew from two herbs of a kind, a second ingredient, a vial, and clean water carried. Drink healing, stamina (a gold buff in the row), or mana; the empty vial comes back. Read: [[Potions]], [[Cauldron]], [[The witch's cauldron]], [[Clay vial]], [[Herbs]].
- ☐ **Mana and Pray at the altar** (game #488, merged 2026-10-26): A blue mana bar sits under stamina. Cast the Fireball: it costs mana on release; with too little you read "Not enough mana." and the bar flashes. Pray at the chapel's altar (a short bar) to fill mana and stamina. Read: [[Mana]], [[Altar]], [[Fireball]].
- ☐ **The fireball as a flare** (game #164, merged 2026-10-17): At night, cast the Fireball (staff and Book of Fire): it lights 6 tiles round it in flight and leaves a 7-tile flare where it lands, fading over 3 seconds. Landing within a tile of a campfire with fuel, or a torch on a post that is out, lights it. Read: [[Fireball]], [[Light]].

## The dead

- ☐ **Burn the dead for a flame alone** (game #508, merged 2026-10-26): With a tinderbox in the bag and no log, right-click a body of the dead and choose Burn corpse: after the two-second bar it burns, and nothing is spent. Light a torch, put the tinderbox in a chest, and with the torch in hand choose Burn corpse on another body: it works too. Then Use the lit torch on a body: it catches at once. An unlit torch Used on a body says "The torch is not lit.", and with neither flame the entry reads "Burn corpse (need a tinderbox or a lit torch)". Hover the entry: its card says "Needs a tinderbox carried or a lit torch in hand; nothing is spent." Read: [[Corpses]], [[Torch]], [[Tinderbox]].
- ☐ **Sight-pull: the dead call the dead** (game #504, merged 2026-10-26): Find three or four of the dead standing together by day. Throw a knife or loose an arrow at one in the middle: it says "Hrraagh!", and the others within about 4 tiles that can see it show a "!" and come for you. Then hit one at the edge of a group with a wall or a corner between it and the rest, or one in the dark at night (the call reaches only about a tile and a half there): it comes alone. A killing blow calls no one, and the ones drawn in never call others. Read: [[Sight-pull]], [[The dead]], [[Combat]], [[Archery]], [[Levers]].
- ☐ **Burn a body of the dead** (game #434, merged 2026-10-25): With a tinderbox (no log since #508, below), right-click a body of the dead and choose Burn corpse (greyed with what you lack otherwise). After a two-second bar it burns with light for about 30 seconds, then it and its contents are gone. Search is greyed while it burns. Read: [[Corpses]], [[Tinderbox]], [[Burning]].
- ☐ **Open a struck gate, they walk in** (game #397, merged 2026-10-24): While the dead strike a fence gate, open it: they stop striking and walk through. Read: [[Fences]].
- ☐ **The dead lunge when close** (game #394, merged 2026-10-24): Let one of the dead chase you: within about 3 tiles it walks 1.5 times as fast to close in. Walking away still outruns it. A trial; Michael may switch it off (burstSpeed 1). Read: [[The dead]], [[Levers]].
- ☐ **Waiting dead strike the fence** (game #389, merged 2026-10-24): Stand behind a fence with several of the dead on the other side: those not biting strike the fence or gate instead of standing still. Read: [[Fences]], [[The dead]].
- ☐ **Only a few bite at once** (game #374, merged 2026-10-24): Let five or six of the dead surround you: only two or three bite at once while the rest wait. A shove or a knockdown passes the turn to another. Read: [[The dead]].
- ☐ **The groan in the dark** (game #173, #189, merged 2026-10-17): At night, with the dead nearby but hidden by the dark, the chat log says "Something groans in the dark." only for those within 6 tiles, at most once every 20 seconds. By day, one hidden behind a tree's canopy never triggers it. Read: [[Chat log]], [[Sight]].
- ☐ **The dead strike and break fences** (game #152, #185, merged 2026-10-17): Stand inside a fenced yard with the dead hunting you. They strike the fence, gate, door, or shutters in their way with a small jolt each time. One alone breaks a stretch of fence in about two and a half minutes, and a crowd breaks it faster, into splinters. Read: [[Fences]], [[Breaking in]], [[The dead]].
- ☐ **Darkness shortens your sight** (game #150, #173, merged 2026-10-17): At night with no torch, the dead fade out past about a tile and a half. With a lit torch in hand you see them out to about 3 tiles, and with one on the belt a little farther than none. A dead one standing in a lit room or a fire's light is seen from any distance. Read: [[Sight]], [[Torch]], [[Light]].
- ☐ **The dead besiege your house** (game #151, #171, merged 2026-10-17): At night, let one of the dead see you go into a house, then shut the door. It waits about the house at the doors and windows it saw you use until dawn (6 o'clock), and the bed refuses: "The dead that know you are in here are still about." Read: [[The dead]], [[Breaking in]], [[Sleep]].
- ☐ **A fence-pressing zombie still bites** (game #171, merged 2026-10-17): While one of the dead is pressing a fence, step right up to the fence on your side: it bites you across it. Read: [[Fences]], [[The dead]].
- ☐ **Nobody climbs through windows** (game #140, merged 2026-10-17): Stand at an open window and press or hold E: there is no climb any more. Shut yourself in at night with the dead outside: they batter a window's shutters to look in, then come round to a door. Read: [[Openings]], [[Breaking in]], [[The dead]], [[Controls]].

## Food & body

- ☐ **Boil a bucket, pour it into skins** (game #508, merged 2026-10-26): Fill the bucket at a well (tainted), then at a lit fireplace or campfire choose Boil water in the bucket on the fire's menu, Boil water on the bucket's own menu, or Use the bucket on the fire: a 60-second bar, then "The water boils. Read: [[Water]], [[Waterskin]], [[Sick]], [[Thirst]], [[Infection]].
- ☐ **Bleeding in the debuff row** (game #315, #320, merged 2026-10-20): Get bitten: a red drop shows in a row at the top right, with its time left and its stacks from two on (a big red badge). ## Building & home

Rulings and ideas from Michael's playtests that change nothing in the game yet. Each lives on the game's GitHub (its issue number below) and in its decisions log, `docs/02`.

- **Throwing potions** (game #483): a potion or a poison held in a hand and thrown, shattering in a splash that works on everyone in it. Ruled, not built.
- **The halberd's thrust and the outline** (game #503): the Swing targets outline shows the sweep's reach and arc, so about a third of the outlined targets are missed by the thrust, and a body straight ahead past the sweep's reach is hit by the thrust with no outline. Filed for Michael to rule which outline a weapon with two modes shows. Read: [[Halberd]].
- **Honey in a clay jar** (game #500, ruled 2026-10-26): Take honey will need an empty clay jar, made as a vial is, and eating the honey will leave the jar; your cottage will start with a couple. Ruled, not built. Read: [[Pot of honey]], [[Beehive]].
- **The art owed for the throwing knife and axe** (game #493): their spin frames and their portraits for the hand slots. Until they come, the throws turn the drawn art step by step and a hand slot shows the bag's icon.
- **Food rots** (ruled): rot is pacing, like a torch burning down, and not decay of the world; how fast, and what preserves food, are in the levers (game #264).
- **The valley's day-one food** is a statistic the wiki keeps current: [[Day-one food]]. Today it would keep one player from starving for about {{day-one-food n=days}} game days, far more than intended; a leaner start and rot are the answers (game #264).
- **Health stays a hundred** (ruled 2026-10-19, withdrawing the day before's ten-times scale): a player starts at a hundred, which levels and gear may raise; the dead and bosses scale as far as they need (game #133, #275).
- **Skills are being shaped** (game #275): ten levels each, unlocks at most levels and a capstone at ten, death taking them.
- **Gear can drop by chance** (ruled), a boss's best item not every kill; **regions should keep drawing players back** (Michael's hope); **the least armour is just better than nothing**; **weapons differ by situation** through resistances (game #133, #123).
- **The map redrawn** with distinct areas and roomier buildings, and **a furniture and rugs pass** (game #92, #261).
- **The regions feed the skills,** a rule drafted for Michael to edit (game #123).
- **Villagers who flee, follow, and stay**, on routines (game #103), and the stretch goals (game #106).

Done since they were filed: **sight-pull** (game #492, built in #504; see [[Sight-pull]]), **the loot tables** (game #268: a table per kind of container, and gear placed once; every table is on [[Drop tables]]) and **bags and stacking** (game #260: one thing a cell by default, the [[Burlap sack]], the [[Linen satchel]], and the [[Rucksack]]).
