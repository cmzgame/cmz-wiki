Each time the wiki is synced with the game, an entry goes here, newest first, with the pages it touched. The game itself is in development; its own decision log is `docs/02-decisions.md` in the game's repository.

## 2026-10-17: synced to `cf81e0d`

- **New on the wiki:** [[Production chains]], a page generated from the game's data each build: every chain from raw material to product, each material a chip joined by arrows, marked works (can be had in normal play), blocked (in the game, but cannot be reached), or planned (in a design doc, not built yet, with the doc and section). It works out what can be had from the starter kit, the loot the valley's containers and the dead roll, the dead's clothes, and felling trees, then every recipe whose inputs, tool, station, and fire can be had; and lists the dead ends, the stations, the skills and the villagers the plan names for them, food, and item art. Linked from the [[Main page]], [[Recipes]], [[Crafting]], the sidebar, and the item and station navboxes.
- **Changed in the game's docs, nothing visible in play:** Michael's rulings for the valley first (game #178): food is to heal a little and only potions much, a villager who teaches a skill is to be immune to the dead by a flag on each villager, a friendly wizard is to teach magic and potions, the dead are to strike what they break, and recipes are to be learned and earned rather than all known from the start. None is built yet, so no page changes; [[Production chains]] shows the skills, the wizard, and the planned villagers as planned.
- On the wiki: the census also reads the starter kit (from `src/game.js` until the game makes it data) and what felling a tree drops, for the new page.

## 2026-10-17: synced to `64cc711`

- **Changed in the game, corrected here:** lighting a fire that is out from its own menu needs a flame (game #169). A campfire, a hearth, a fireplace, an [[Oven]], or a [[Forge]] that is out is lit from its menu only with a [[Tinderbox]] anywhere you carry it, or a lit [[Torch]] in a hand or on the belt, as well as its fuel; without one the entry reads "(need a tinderbox or a flame)", `E` passes it by, and a click says "You need a tinderbox or a lit torch to light it." Feeding a fire that is burning needs no flame. [[Fire]] and [[Tinderbox]] say how; [[Oven]], [[Forge]], [[Torch]], [[Belt]], [[Controls]], [[Your cottage]], and [[Getting started]] corrected.
- **Changed in the game, corrected here:** loose ends of fences and the siege (game #171). One of the dead pressing a fence bites a player within reach across it. It no longer gives up pressing while it knows the player is in a room, by day or night, so a house in a fenced yard is no safe place; only on a player last seen out in the open does it give up, after {{n tuning.zombie.pressGiveUp seconds}} unseen. The door the dead remember in a siege is the one on the line from where they last saw you outside, so with two doors close together it is the one you used. A player standing right at an open window can be bitten from outside it. "A torch burns out." reaches players within {{n tuning.world.torchSpentRadius tiles}}. [[Fences]], [[The dead]], [[Breaking in]], [[Openings]], [[Torch]], and [[Torch posts and brackets]] corrected.
- **Changed in the game, corrected here:** a torch in a wall bracket works as one on a post (game #172). A [[Use]] can aim at a wall bracket: a tinderbox or a lit torch used on a bracket's torch that is out lights it, and an unlit torch in your hand or on your belt used on a lit one is lit from it, never through its wall. A Use on a lantern says "A lantern is always lit.", and on an empty bracket "There is no torch there." [[Torch posts and brackets]], [[Use]], [[Tinderbox]], [[Torch]], and [[Fire]] corrected.
- **Changed in the game, corrected here:** the dark's loose ends (game #173). "Something groans in the dark." comes only for one of the dead the dark hides, never one behind a tree's canopy by day. A torch on the belt now gives {{n tuning.world.beltLight}} of a held torch's light and reach, a little past the {{n tuning.sight.darkRadius tiles}} you see in the dark without one, so it lets you see a little farther, and the dead see its flame from a little farther too. Sight's thresholds are data: one of the dead counts as seen from {{n tuning.sight.seenAt}} of whole, is not drawn below {{n tuning.sight.drawCutoff}}, and the groan line comes at most every {{n tuning.sight.groanGap seconds}}. [[Sight]], [[Chat log]], [[Light]], [[Belt]], [[Torch]], and [[The dead]] corrected.
- On the wiki: the pages of the last sync had their Sources moved on to the new game; [[Sight]], [[Chat log]], [[Oven]], and [[Forge]] were rechecked whole.

## 2026-10-17: synced to `2ea0d24`

- **New in the game:** the light verb (game #165). A [[Tinderbox]] or a lit [[Torch]] [[Use|used]] on a campfire that is out but has fuel lights it ("You light the fire."), and used on a torch post's torch that is out, or an unlit torch in your hand or on your belt, lights that ("You light the torch."). ## 2026-10-17: synced to `2a97f49`

- **New in the game:** the siege (game #151). At night, from {{n census.constants.clock.DUSK_START}} o'clock, one of the dead that sees you inside a room it is not in holds a siege on you until {{n tuning.zombie.siegeEndHour}} o'clock: it remembers the door it saw you go in by and any window it saw you through, and breaks in there first. When its search finds no one it no longer gives up but waits about the house, going between the openings it knows, and with no way in at all it waits at the one it saw you by. At dawn it loses interest and wanders off; by day a sighting is the ordinary chase. You cannot sleep while one holding a siege on you is within {{n tuning.zombie.siegeSleepRadius tiles}} ("The dead that know you are in here are still about."); a sleep already begun is not broken. Nothing calls more of the dead. A room with no door is never entered, and cannot be walked out of. [[Breaking in]] has a new section on the siege; [[The dead]], [[Sleep]], [[Rooms]], [[The day]], and [[Getting started]] say what it changes.
- **New in the game:** a crowd of the dead bursts a fence (game #152). One pressing a fence does nothing; {{n tuning.world.fenceCrowd}} or more wear it down, faster the more there are, and it bursts into splinters; a gate gives way at {{n tuning.world.fenceGateFactor}} of a fence's wear and the dead seek it first. They press a fence between them and a player they cannot walk to, in a chase and in a siege, so a house in a fenced yard is no safe place. The fence shakes while pressed; a burst stretch is gone. The dead still bite over a fence, and you can still hit them over one. [[Fences]] is rewritten round it, keeping its section on fighting across a fence; [[The dead]], [[Breaking in]], and [[Walls]] corrected.
- **New in the game:** doors, windows, and shutters built onto walls (game #155). A door or a window can be built over any plain wall, the village's own included, in that wall's material, and a door over a fence as a gate; removing it puts the wall back, still yours to remove if it was. A new door can be built over a broken door, and a new window over broken shutters, whole and shut. Every door and every window can be broken by the dead, whatever its wall is made of. [[Building]] has a new section; [[Openings]], [[Walls]], [[Fences]], and [[Breaking in]] no longer say nothing can be built into a broken opening.
- **New in the game:** torches burn down, and the tinderbox (game #157). A torch is lit or out, and a new one, the starting torch included, is out. `T` lights the torch in hand, or else on the belt, with a tinderbox carried, and douses a lit one. A lit torch burns down: it lasts {{n tuning.world.torchHeldLife seconds}} of the world's time in a hand and {{n tuning.world.torchWallLife seconds}} on the belt, in a bracket, or on a post, a kill wearing the same meter; spent, it is gone ("Your torch is spent.", or "A torch burns out." for a mounted one). A torch put away, dropped, or taken from a holder goes out. Only a lit torch gives light or sets the dead alight. Every new character has a tinderbox in the bag. A new page, [[Tinderbox]]; [[Torch]] rewritten; [[Torch posts and brackets]], [[Light]], [[Fire]], [[Belt]], [[Controls]], [[Player]], [[Getting started]], [[Developer chest]], [[Durability]], [[Burning]], and [[The day]] corrected.
- **Changed in the game, corrected here:** the village's lights are lanterns, and the fireball is a flare (game #164). Every light the village had in a bracket, and the square's three posts, is a lantern now: always lit, steady, out to {{n sprites.lantern_post.fire.radius tiles}}, never burning down, and not to be taken; the four empty brackets are left for a torch of your own. The [[Fireball]] lights {{n combat.bow.fireballGlow tiles}} round it as it flies and leaves a flare of {{n combat.bow.flareRadius tiles}} where it comes down, fading over {{n combat.bow.flareSeconds seconds}}; landing within reach of a campfire with fuel or a mounted torch that is out, with no wall between, it lights it, never a hearth, fireplace, oven, or forge; its damage is now {{n items.book_of_fire.book.spell.damage}}. A torch on a belt or in a bag that is dropped goes out, and so does every torch on a body. [[Torch posts and brackets]] rewritten; [[Fireball]], [[Light]], [[Fire]], [[Walls]], [[Torch]], [[Belt]], [[The inn]], [[The toll house]], and [[Your cottage]] corrected.
- **Changed in the game, said here:** the big line of the game's name on its site is zombie green (game #163; [[Screens]]).
- Not visible to a player: Michael's rulings on fire and torches (game #153, built by #157 and #164 above) and his roadmap with the sound rulings (game #160; the wiki's own rule for sounds is in `WRITING.md`).
- On the wiki: the pages above, and [[The chapel]] and [[The crypt]], had their Sources rechecked against the new game.
