**The orchard** is a field of apple trees on grass in the south-west of [[The valley]], west of the lane's last cottages. Its trees stand in staggered rows, and two barrels stand at its southern edge. It has no buildings and no fence. It is the west end of [[Hollow Farm]]. West of it are the farm's two fenced pens, [[Hollow Farm's byre]] with its cows and [[Hollow Farm's pigsty]] with its pigs (see [[Animals]]). North of it, on the wood's edge, stands [[Hollow Farm's dovecote]], a small round stone room.

Each tree is a green round tree with red apples through its crown, picked by hand for [[Apple|apples]]; it then stands bare, the same crown without its apples, for some days before it bears again (see [[Trees#Fruit trees|Trees]]). Three cottage gardens have an apple tree of their own: the cottage by the merchant's, the cottage by the baker's, and the first cottage on the lane.

## The dead

None of the dead are placed in the orchard.

## With friends

A tree picked by one player is bare for everyone, and bears again for everyone on the same day.
