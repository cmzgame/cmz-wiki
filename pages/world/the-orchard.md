**The orchard** is a field of apple trees on grass in the south-west of [[The valley]], west of the lane's last cottages. Its trees stand in staggered rows, and two barrels stand at its southern edge. It has no buildings and no fence. It is the first part of Hollow Farm, the big farm the game's plan puts here; the rest of the farm is not built yet.

Each tree is picked by hand for [[Apple|apples]] and then stands bare for some days before it bears again (see [[Trees#Fruit trees|Trees]]). Three cottage gardens have an apple tree of their own: the cottage by the merchant's, the cottage by the baker's, and the first cottage on the lane.

## The dead

None of the dead are placed in the orchard.

## With friends

A tree picked by one player is bare for everyone, and bears again for everyone on the same day.
