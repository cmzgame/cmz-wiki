**The smithy** stands south-east of the square, near the river. The forge room has a stone floor, the [[Forge]], the [[Anvil]], a grindstone, a barrel, a shelf, and a chest. Its two doors on the south side stand open at the start. The forge starts unlit; it burns [[Charcoal]], not logs (see [[Fire]]). Beside it is the smith's home, a small room with a bed, a chest, and a cupboard. Outside are a woodpile and a cart.

{{place-contents "the smithy"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands outside the smithy's open doors at the start.
