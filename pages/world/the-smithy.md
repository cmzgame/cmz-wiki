**The smithy** stands south-east of the square, near the river. The forge room has a stone floor, the [[Forge]], the [[Anvil]], a grindstone, a barrel, a shelf, and a chest that holds the first [[Iron bar|iron bars]] and some [[Charcoal]]. Its two doors on the south side stand open at the start. The forge starts unlit; it burns [[Charcoal]], not logs (see [[Fire]]). Beside it is the smith's home, a small room with a bed, a chest, and a cupboard. Outside are a woodpile, a cart, and a [[Charcoal clamp]] for burning logs into the forge's charcoal.

## The dead

One of the dead stands outside the smithy's open doors at the start.
