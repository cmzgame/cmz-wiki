**Long Acre** is the farm in the north of [[The valley]], off the north lane, with the brook along its north edge. Its fields grow the crops the village cannot: **rye** in two fields, its harvest [[Grain]] for the [[Scythe]] and [[The mill]], and a strip of [[Flax]] for the [[Loom]].

## What is there

- **The farmhouse** and **the barn**, with a well and haystacks in the yard between them.
- **The west rye** and **the east rye**, two fields with furrows running north and south.
- **The flax** strip and **the kitchen garden**, by the brook.

Each is a place of its own on the map: [[Long Acre's farmhouse]], [[Long Acre's barn]], [[Long Acre's west rye]], [[Long Acre's east rye]], [[Long Acre's flax]], and [[Long Acre's kitchen garden]].

North of the farm the **brook** runs east to the river; it is a water of its own for [[Fishing]]. The lane to the mere crosses it on a plank footbridge.

## The dead

Four of the dead: two in the rye, one in the yard, one in the barn.
