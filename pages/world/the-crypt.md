**The crypt** is a small stone building in the north-east corner of the graveyard, near [[The chapel]]. It is one room with a door on the west side and no windows. Inside are two coffins and a chest. Its one wall bracket is empty, so it is dark inside unless you bring a light (see [[Torch posts and brackets]]).

{{place-contents "the crypt"}}

## The dead

None of the dead start inside the crypt, but the graveyard round it is full of them.
