**The merchant's house** stands on the west side of the nobles' street. The house is stone, split into rooms by partitions: a living room with a fireplace, a table and chairs; a counting room with a desk, a strongbox, and a bookcase; and a bedroom. Doors open to the east and south.

South of the house is a fenced yard with a gate to the east, a cart, and a wooden storehouse lined with shelves, barrels, chests, and a cupboard.

The [[Apprentice]] stands in the storehouse.

{{place-contents "the merchant's house"}}

## The dead

One of the dead starts inside the house, and another in the storehouse with the Apprentice.
