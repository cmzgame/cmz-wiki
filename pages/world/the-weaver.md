**The weaver** is a wooden house west of the square, on the west road. Its workroom holds the [[Loom]] and the [[Spinning wheel]], the valley's only one, which spins [[Wool]] into yarn for the loom's [[Woollen cloth]]; a partition closes off a room with a bed, a chest, and a fireplace. The chest holds flax seed and a [[Dirk]], so cloth can be begun on the first day (see [[Flax]] and [[Linen cloth]]). The door faces south. In the yard to the east stand three [[Dye vat|dye vats]], where clothes are dyed, and a woodpile.
