**The weaver** is a wooden house west of the square, on the west road. Its workroom holds the [[Loom]] and a spinning wheel; a partition closes off a room with a bed, a chest, and a fireplace. The door faces south. In the yard to the east stand three dye vats (see [[Dye vat]]) and a woodpile.

{{place-contents "the weaver"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands in the yard at the start.
