**The iron workings** are the valley's mine, in the east woods: a low cliff of stone along the north with the mine's mouth in it, spoil heaps and a winch, a plank tramway, and an ore cart. Six ore rocks stand about it, mined with a [[Pickaxe]] for [[Iron ore]]; each stands mined out for some days, then bears ore again (see [[Mining]]). The ore cart holds iron ore from the start.

East of it is **[[The miners' hut|the miners' hut]]**, with two beds, a table, a barrel, and a chest that holds a pickaxe and some iron ore, so the mine can be worked on arriving. [[The charcoal burners]] are just to the south.

## The dead

Some of the dead walk about the workings, and one stands by the miners' hut.
