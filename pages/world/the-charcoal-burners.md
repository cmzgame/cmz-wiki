**The charcoal burners** work a black clearing in the east woods, south of [[The iron workings]]. Three [[Charcoal clamp|charcoal clamps]] stand in a row, turning logs into [[Charcoal]], with two sacks of charcoal and a wheelbarrow. Beside the clearing is **[[The burners' hut|the burners' hut]]**, with a bed, a barrel, and a chest of charcoal.
