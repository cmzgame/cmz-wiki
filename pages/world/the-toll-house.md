**The toll house** is a small wooden house south of the high street, near the west end of the stone bridge. Inside are a bed, a chest, a shelf, and a strongbox. The door is on the west side, and a torch burns in a bracket on the outside of that wall, lighting the road at night.

{{place-contents "the toll house"}}

## The dead

None of the dead start inside. A knot of them stands to the north at the bridge's west end, by the toll gate, and more at the bridge's far end.
