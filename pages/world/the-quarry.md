**The quarry** is a pit in the north-east of the valley, cut into stone on three sides and open to the south. Eight rocks stand on its dirt floor, mined with a [[Pickaxe]] for [[Stone]] (see [[Mining]]), and four stacks of cut stone along the north face are searched like a chest. There are a crane, a wheelbarrow, and a small flooded pit, a pond (see [[Fishing]]).

South-west of it is **[[The quarryman's hut|the quarryman's hut]]**, with a bed, a table, a barrel, and a chest that holds a pickaxe and a [[Smith's hammer]].

## The dead

Some of the dead walk the quarry floor, and one stands by the hut.
