{{infobox-place "the joiner"}}

**The joiner** is south-west of the square. The workshop holds a [[Workbench]], a table, shelves, a barrel, and a chest that always holds a [[Hatchet]] and a [[Smith's hammer]], the tools the workbench's rows ask for, with its door on the east side. Behind it is the joiner's home, with a fireplace, a table, a bed, and a chest. South of the buildings is the lumber yard: a sawpit, two woodpiles, and a wheelbarrow.

{{place-contents "the joiner"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands just outside the workshop's door at the start.
