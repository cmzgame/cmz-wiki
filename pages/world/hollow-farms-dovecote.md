**Hollow Farm's dovecote** is a small round stone building on the edge of the wood north of [[The orchard]], part of [[Hollow Farm]]. It is the valley's one [[Round rooms|round room]], {{n valley.sets.49.rounds.0.size}} tiles across, its curved stone wall drawn as a ring round a stone floor.

## What is there

An empty round room: a door on the south side, toward the farm, and a window on the east side, each at one of the ring's door points and drawn in the curve in an arched frame of dressed stone: a curved door, and arched shutters. A stone skirt runs round the foot of the wall inside. Nothing stands inside it yet.

It cannot be taken apart: it is part of the map. Inside, the stretch of its near half between you and the room is cut away to one faded face, its ends closed by the wall's thickness, the flanks staying solid, and the floor under the cut is one stone base with the skirt; the window on the east side fades with the cut. With its door shut it is a room like any other (see [[Round rooms]]).

## The dead

None are placed in or round it.
