**Hollow Farm's dovecote** is a small round stone building on the edge of the wood north of [[The orchard]], part of [[Hollow Farm]]. It is the valley's one [[Round rooms|round room]], {{n valley.sets.49.rounds.0.size}} tiles across, its curved stone wall drawn as a ring round a stone floor.

## What is there

An empty round room: a door on the south side, toward the farm, and a window on the east side, each at one of the ring's door points and drawn in the curve, with no straight frame. A stone skirt runs round the foot of the wall inside. Nothing stands inside it yet.

It cannot be taken apart: it is part of the map. Inside, the stretch of its near half between you and the room fades as a room's near walls do, the flanks staying solid, and with its door shut it is a room like any other (see [[Round rooms]]).

## The dead

None are placed in or round it.
