{{infobox-prop altar}}

The **altar** stands at the north end of [[The chapel]], before the pews. It is the one place to **Pray**, which fills your [[Mana]] and your [[Stamina]] after a short bar. It is drawn as a placeholder box labelled "Altar", and it cannot be carried.

## Praying

Right-click the altar and choose **Pray**. You walk to it if you are not already within reach, then a bar of {{n tuning.potions.praySeconds seconds}} runs over it. When the bar is done, your mana and your stamina are both full, and you are no longer winded: "You pray at the altar. Your mana and stamina are restored."

- It is a menu entry only: `E` beside the altar does not pray.
- Walking, or moving out of reach, ends the bar, and nothing is filled.
- It is refused with your arms full of furniture ("Your hands are full.").
- It has no cooldown, and it may be done with the bars already full. The walk to the chapel is its cost.

The game's data records Faith {{n sprites.altar.pray.level}} for praying; the skills are not built, so no one is refused.

{{prop-sections altar}}

## The sound it makes

A rising note as the bar begins, heard only by the one praying; the bar over the altar and then the filled bars are its picture. It is a placeholder generated by the game. See [[Sound]].

## With friends

A guest prays through the same menu entry, sent to the host by its label with the altar's id. The host runs the bar for that player, with the same reach and the same refusals, and fills that player's mana and stamina at its end; the guest sees the bar over the altar and their own bars fill. Two players may pray at once.
