**The manor** is a stone house in a walled garden on the east side of the nobles' street, the largest house in the village. It has three rooms: the hall, with a fireplace, a table and chairs, and a cupboard; the kitchen to the south of it, with a second fireplace; and the solar and bedroom to the east, with the bed, a bookcase, a desk, and a strongbox. Both fireplaces start unlit. The doors are on the west and south sides, with windows on three sides, and two lanterns hang in brackets inside.

South of the house is the garden, fenced with gates to the west and east. It holds two benches, three [[Beehive|beehives]] (drawn straw skeps, each with a [[Pot of honey]] to take, and more {{n tuning.work.honey.days}} game days after), a small pond, and a trough to drink from (see [[Water]]).

{{place-contents "the manor"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands in the garden at the start, and more walk the nobles' street outside.
