**Your cottage** is the end cottage on the south lane of [[The valley]], and the place where every new character starts, standing inside. It is a small wooden house of two rooms: a main room with a fireplace, a table and two chairs, and a chest, and a bedroom behind a partition with the bed, a chest, and a shelf. The door is on the south side, with windows to the south, north, and east, and a torch burns in a bracket on the inside of the west wall. The fireplace starts unlit; feed it a [[Log]] to light it (see [[Fire]]).

Round the house is a fenced garden with gates to the south and west. In it are a farm plot of potatoes and cabbages (see [[Crops]]), a well to drink from and fill [[Waterskin|waterskins]] at (see [[Water]]), a barrel, and a woodpile. The bed is where you [[Sleep]].

{{place-contents "your cottage"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands just inside the garden's north fence at the start. More walk the lanes to the west and north.
