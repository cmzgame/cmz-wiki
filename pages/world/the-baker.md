**The baker** is a small wooden shop at the north-west corner of the square. The shop has the [[Oven]] in its wall, a table, a shelf, and a barrel, and a small room beside it, behind a partition, holds a bed and a chest. The door and a window face north. The oven is lit at the start with {{n valley.sets.9.buildings.0.props.0.fuel}} logs, so it can bake on the first morning before it needs feeding (see [[Fire]] and [[Cooking]]).

{{place-contents "the baker"}}

## The dead

One of the dead stands at the shop's door at the start.
