**The inn** stands on the east side of the square in the middle of the village. Its main building holds the taproom, with a counter, barrels, and tables and chairs, and the kitchen behind a partition. Both rooms have a fireplace, and both are lit at the start with {{n valley.sets.1.buildings.0.props.16.fuel}} logs each, so the inn is warm and bright on the first day until they burn out (see [[Fire]]). Three of the taproom's windows are boarded from the inside (see [[Openings]]). A torch burns over the door outside, on the west wall, and more inside.

South of the taproom is the guest wing: three small rooms, each with its own door off the taproom, a bed, and a chest. Behind the inn is a fenced yard with a gate to the west, holding a well, a trough, a woodpile, a cart, and a privy.

The [[Innkeeper]] stands in the taproom, the [[Child]] nearby, and the [[Guest]] in the guest wing.

{{place-contents "the inn"}}

## The dead

The dead at the west end of the bridge, a knot of them by the toll gate, stand on the road just east of the inn's taproom, inside its ground. None start inside the building.
