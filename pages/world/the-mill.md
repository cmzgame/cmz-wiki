**The mill** stands on the river's west bank at the end of the north lane, its wheel against the river. Inside are the [[Millstones]], which grind [[Grain]] into [[Flour]], with barrels, a chest, and a shelf. The [[Miller]] is there, alive. Beside it, [[The mill's granary]] has a chest of seed of every crop, never refilled.
